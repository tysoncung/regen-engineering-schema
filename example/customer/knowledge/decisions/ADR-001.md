## Decision

Deleting a customer marks the record deleted and retains it. It does not remove
rows.

## Why

Order history must survive customer deletion for the legal retention period,
and orders reference the customer. A hard delete would either orphan orders or
cascade into destroying financial records.

## Alternatives considered

- **Hard delete with anonymisation.** Rejected: irreversible, and an
  anonymisation bug is unrecoverable.
- **Hard delete with archival copy.** Rejected: two sources of truth for the
  same customer, which is the drift this methodology exists to avoid.

## Consequences

- Email addresses are never released for reuse, see [BR-001](../rules/BR-001.md).
- Authentication must explicitly reject deleted accounts, see [BR-003](../rules/BR-003.md).
- A purge job outside this module handles the end of the retention period.
