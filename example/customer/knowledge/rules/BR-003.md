---
id: BR-003
type: business-rule
title: Deleted customers cannot authenticate
status: active
since: 2026-07-30
affects: [customer]
implemented_by: [customer]
verified_by: [CT-003]
---

A customer whose account is deleted cannot authenticate, and any existing
session is invalidated at the moment of deletion.

Deletion is soft: the record and its order history are retained for the legal
retention period. Authentication attempts against a deleted account return the
same generic failure as a wrong password, so account existence is not disclosed.
