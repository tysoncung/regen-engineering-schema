id: BR-001

title: Customer email must be unique
status: active
since: 2026-07-30
affects: [customer]
implemented_by: [customer]
verified_by: [CT-001]
---

An email address identifies at most one customer account.

Comparison is case-insensitive and ignores surrounding whitespace. Registration
with an email already in use is rejected, and the rejection must not reveal
whether the existing account belongs to someone else.

Deleted customers continue to hold their email, so it cannot be reused. See
[ADR-001](../decisions/ADR-001.md) for why deletion is soft.
