## Scenario: registering an email already in use

Given a customer registered with `ada@example.com`
When someone registers with `ada@example.com`
Then registration is rejected
And the response does not disclose that an account already exists

## Scenario: uniqueness ignores case and surrounding whitespace

Given a customer registered with `ada@example.com`
When someone registers with `  ADA@Example.com  `
Then registration is rejected

## Scenario: a deleted customer's email stays taken

Given a customer registered with `ada@example.com` whose account is deleted
When someone registers with `ada@example.com`
Then registration is rejected
