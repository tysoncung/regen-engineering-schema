## Scenario: authentication after deletion

Given a customer with valid credentials whose account is deleted
When the customer attempts to authenticate with those credentials
Then authentication fails
And the failure is indistinguishable from a wrong password

## Scenario: existing sessions end at deletion

Given a customer with an active session
When the account is deleted
Then the existing session no longer authorises requests
