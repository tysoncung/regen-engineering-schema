Deleted customer records are retained for seven years.

This is believed, not verified. It came from a conversation with finance in the
context of one jurisdiction, and no legal review has confirmed it applies
everywhere the product operates.

If it is wrong, [ADR-001](../decisions/ADR-001.md) still holds, but the purge
schedule changes. Recorded here so that the next person does not mistake it for
an established fact.
