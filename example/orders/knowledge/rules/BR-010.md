---
id: BR-010
type: business-rule
title: An order requires a resolvable shipping address
status: active
since: 2026-07-30
affects: [orders, customer]
implemented_by: [orders]
verified_by: [CT-010]
---

An order cannot be placed unless the customer has at least one address, and the
order captures a copy of the address used rather than a reference to it.

The copy matters: a customer who later edits or deletes that address must not
retroactively change where a past order was sent.

This rule spans two modules. It depends on the default-address behaviour in
[BR-002](../../../customer/knowledge/rules/BR-002.md), which is why a change to
that rule puts `orders` inside the regeneration scope even though the rule lives
in `customer`.
