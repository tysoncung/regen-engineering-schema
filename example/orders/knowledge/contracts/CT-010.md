## Scenario: an order cannot be placed without an address

Given a customer with no addresses
When the customer attempts to place an order
Then the order is rejected

## Scenario: editing an address does not alter past orders

Given a customer who placed an order shipped to their default address
When the customer edits that address
Then the past order still shows the address as it was at placement

## Scenario: deleting an address does not alter past orders

Given a customer who placed an order shipped to an address
When the customer deletes that address
Then the past order still shows the address as it was at placement

## Scenario: placement stays within the latency budget

Given a customer with a default address
When the customer places an order
Then the response completes within 500ms at the 95th percentile
