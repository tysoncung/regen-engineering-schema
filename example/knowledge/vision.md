# Vision

A small commerce system, used here only as a worked example of the Regen
Engineering knowledge schema.

It exists to demonstrate three things that a single-module example cannot:

1. A knowledge package that is complete enough to regenerate a module from
2. A rule that spans two modules, so impact analysis has something real to walk
3. Provenance and drift recorded per module, including one module deliberately
   left in a `knowledge-ahead` state

The domain is deliberately boring. The interesting part is the structure.
